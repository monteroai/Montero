status texts

auto shop

beforeOwners call all day asking "is my car done?"
nowThe shop texts them at every step. The phone goes quiet.
how it's built
  1. One row per car on a shared board: In, Working, Needs OK, Done.
  2. Moving a car to a new column sends the owner a text.
  3. Needs OK sends the photo and the price, and they tap approve.
  4. Done sends a pay link. Once it's paid, they get the lockbox code.
  5. Ask for their OK to text at check-in.

tools: google sheets, zapier, twilio, a stripe link. Many shop programs already have this built in.

pickup slots

deli

beforeEveryone shows up at noon and the line goes out the door.
nowPeople order ahead, pick a time, and grab it off a shelf.
how it's built
  1. Turn on online ordering with pickup times in your register system.
  2. Make 15-minute slots. Each one takes only as many orders as the kitchen can make in 15 minutes.
  3. Tickets print 15 minutes before the slot, not when the order comes in.
  4. Text the customer when it's ready, with a shelf number.

tools: square online, toast or clover, a numbered shelf.
