Status texts

Nobody calls asking "is it done?" The board texts them first.

wiring5 nodes
  1. 1

    A board with one row per car. status: in → working → needs_ok → done

  2. 2

    Status changes, a text goes out. Trigger on the cell, send through a texting service.

    > Your Civic is checked in. Ready by 4:00 PM.
  3. 3

    needs_ok sends the photo and price. A tap approves and moves the card back to working.

    > Front brakes are worn. $240. Tap to approve.
  4. 4

    done sends a pay link. Paid sends the lockbox code.

    > Paid $389. Keys in the lockbox, code 4821.
  5. 5

    Opt-in at check-in. "Reply STOP to opt out" on the first text.

stack: Google Sheets / Zapier or Make / Twilio / Stripe link · or the texting built into your shop software

Pickup slots

The lunch rush, spread into 15-minute pieces the kitchen can actually make.

wiring5 nodes
  1. 1

    Online ordering with scheduled pickup times, from your point-of-sale.

  2. 2

    Slots every 15 minutes. cap = what the kitchen makes in 15 min. Full slot, next one.

  3. 3

    Tickets print 15 minutes before the slot, not when the order lands.

  4. 4

    Ready sends a text with the shelf number.

    > Order 41 is on shelf 3. Walk in, grab it.
  5. 5

    Link on the door, the Google listing and the bio.

stack: Square Online / Toast / Clover / a numbered shelf
